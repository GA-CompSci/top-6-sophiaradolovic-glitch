The Close Contenders

They may not have cracked the top six, but trust me, your tastebuds will still thank you.

Rosebud Steakhouse

Brings timeless, high-quality steakhouse elegance. Consistently delivers on flavor and atmosphere, but it is not as distinctive as my top 6.

Weber Grill

Stands out for its consistency and authentic flame-grilled flavor. The experience is solid from start to finish, however, the classic menu keeps it out of the top 6.

Portillo's

A Chicago Staple. While it's legendary, it's fast-paced comfort vibe kept it out of the top 6.
